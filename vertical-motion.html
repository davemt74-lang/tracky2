HOW TO PLAY

Random Follow Pattern: select 1 or 2 enrolled players, choose a 30/60/90/120-second interval and 5/10/15/20 total rounds. Each interval is one round. The active player follows random zones on the shared four-section board, completing 3–6 up/down reps per target. Each finished target earns a point, and new targets continue until the timer expires. The next timed round passes to the next player. Classic three-zone solo and two-player point-goal games remain available in Game mode.

The 1″ × 5″ lane uses CSS physical units. Exact physical size depends on display scaling and monitor calibration.
